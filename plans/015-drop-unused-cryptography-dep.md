## Why this matters

`pyproject.toml` declares **both** `cryptography>=41.0.0` and
`pycryptodome>=3.19.0`. Only `pycryptodome` (imported as `Crypto.*`) is used in
the codebase — `cryptography` has **zero source imports** anywhere in the repo.
It pulls in `cffi` and `pycparser` transitively, adding ~4.7MB of compiled
wheels to every `uv sync` / `pip install` for no functional reason. For a
tool whose job is auditing security posture, an unused crypto dependency is
needless attack surface.
