## Why this matters

`generate_report.py` is a standalone report generator that hardcodes a
`MODULE_SUMMARIES` dict. It includes a `'new_attacks'` entry claiming 5 attacks,
2 successful — but **no `new_attacks` module exists in the framework**. The five
`.py` files under `prompt-inj-attacks/new_attacks/` have zero importers anywhere
in the repo. The fabricated `new_attacks` row propagates into the published HTML/JSON/MD
reports as a **HIGH-severity OWASP "LL00 Unclassified" finding** (40% success rate).
A security-testing tool publishing phantom HIGH findings undermines trust in
every other row of the report.
